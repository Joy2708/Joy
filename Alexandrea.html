Alexandria

Alexandria is a major Egyptian port city on the Mediterranean Sea. Founded by Alexander the Great in 331 BCE, it was once a famous center of learning and home to the ancient Lighthouse and Great Library. Today, it is a bustling hub known for its rich history, seaside promenade, and landmarks like the Citadel of Qaitbay.
