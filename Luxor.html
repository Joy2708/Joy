Luxor

luxor is a historic city in upper Egypt situated on the Nile River, famous as the site of ancient Thebes. Often called the world's greatest open-air museum, it houses iconic landmarks including the Luxor Temple, Karnak Temple, and the royal tombs in the Valley of the Kings.
