my fav places to see in egypt.

paramidys

The Egyptian pyramids are monumental ancient masonry structures built primarily as tombs for pharaohs and their consorts. Over 100 have been identified, with the most iconic being the three grand pyramids of Giza (Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure) located on the outskirts of Cairo.

khan el khalili

It is Egypt's most famous market. Located in the heart of Islamic Cairo, near the Medieval walled city of the Saladin Citadel, the Khan el-Khalili market was built on the old burial site of the Fatimid Caliphs, who founded Cairo in the 10th century.
